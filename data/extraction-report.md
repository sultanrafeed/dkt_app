## How the data was extracted

- **Questions, options:** text read from every page of `driver-knowledge-test-questions-car.pdf` (94 pages), keeping page breaks together.
- **Official answer:** the source PDF prints the correct option in a bold font (Arial-Black or Arial-BoldMT); distractors are regular weight. Every question has exactly one bold option. The answer is never inferred from position.
- **Images:** every embedded picture and vector drawing between one question heading and the next is cropped from the page at 180 dpi, with the question text removed. Labels printed on the picture (letters such as A, B, M, N, O) are kept. All 193 embedded pictures in the PDF are assigned to exactly one question; one sign (LD033) is vector artwork and is also captured.
- **Handbook:** all 212 pages of `Road-User-Handbook-English.pdf` were read into 78 sections using the handbook's own heading styles. Each page is also rendered as an image, because most handbook diagrams and signs are vector artwork.
- **Handbook links on questions:** chosen automatically by keyword rules (and a text-similarity fallback). They point to relevant reading; they do not change any question or answer.

## Questions requiring manual review

The official answer is kept exactly as printed. These notes flag where the 2018 question document and the February 2026 handbook differ, or where the handbook does not cover the point.

- **AD034** – The question says 'serious crashes'; the 2026 handbook (p. 42) says around 50% of *fatal* crashes on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights involve alcohol. The official answer (about 50%) matches either way.
- **CG045** – The handbook (p. 174) lists name and address, registration number and the owner's name and address; showing your licence is listed for police (p. 175). The official answer is kept.
- **CG062** – Refers to the 'RTA'. The 2026 handbook (p. 24) says you must tell Transport for NSW.
- **CG064** – Refers to the 'RTA', now Transport for NSW (handbook p. 186).
- **CG110** – The handbook covers flashing lights on the top/back of school buses (p. 36, p. 81), not headlights flashing on the front of a bus.
- **ICAC1** – The 6-week re-test ban is stated only in the question document. The 2026 handbook does not mention it (p. 6 only says you must not cheat).
- **IN039** – The 2026 handbook (p. 91) describes red-light *speed* cameras that detect both red-light running and speeding, so the second option is now also partly true for those cameras. The official answer from the 2018 document is kept.
- **ND040** – The handbook (p. 62) lists 'large fines' and 'demerit points' for street racing but does not state a number of demerit points. The '3 demerit points' figure comes only from the 2018 question document.
